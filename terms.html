The service

Sosial is a social-media composer, scheduler and publishing service. You connect your own accounts on third-party platforms, and Sosial publishes the content you schedule through each platform's official API, on your behalf. It also provides a shared calendar, an inbox for messages and comments, an AI writer, approvals and per-channel results.

Connected platforms

Sosial publishes to 18 channels: X, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Threads, YouTube, LinkedIn, Bluesky, Mastodon, Pinterest, Telegram, Discord, WordPress, Dev.to, Hashnode, Ghost, VK and Google Business Profile. Sixteen are live today; LinkedIn and Google Business Profile are in preview and light up once the platform approves our access. Your use of each connected platform remains governed by that platform's own terms and policies. X requires a paid plan because its posting API is metered.

Publishing means posting your scheduled text, photos and video, uploading media where a platform requires it, and reading the minimum profile, delivery-status and statistics fields needed to confirm a post went out and show you results. Each platform shows its exact permissions on its own connect screen before you approve them. Bluesky uses a handle and an app password instead of OAuth; the password is stored encrypted and used only to mint short-lived sessions for publishing.

Messages, comments and approvals

Some channels also let you read and answer messages and comments in Sosial: direct messages on Facebook Pages (Messenger), Instagram, X, Mastodon and Bluesky, and comments on Facebook, Instagram, Threads, X, Mastodon, Bluesky and YouTube. You are responsible for what you send and for each platform's messaging rules.

In a shared workspace the owner decides who is invited and what role they hold. Members can be required to submit posts for approval: a member's post goes to the Approvals queue and an owner or admin must approve it before it publishes. Whoever approves a post takes responsibility for that decision.

Content sources, automation and AI

Media from Dropbox, Canva, Unsplash and OneDrive is read-only: we never change or delete anything in your storage. Imports from Notion and Google Sheets are one-way, into drafts. Your own automations can post through Zapier or MCP with an API key you control. AI features — the writer, per-channel captions, and blog and studio drafts — send your brief to OpenAI to generate a result; we never use your inputs to train models, and you should review AI output before publishing.
