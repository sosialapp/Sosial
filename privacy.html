Privacy Policy

Effective: October 27, 2026 · Sosial (“we”, “the app”), operated by EGATE WORLDWIDE

What Sosial does

Sosial lets you compose, schedule and publish posts to social accounts you connect, share a calendar and approval flow with your team, read and answer messages and comments in one inbox, and draft copy with AI. This policy explains what data that requires and how we handle it.

Data we collect

  • Account info: your email address, used for sign-in and team identity.
  • Workspace data: posts, captions, schedules, media, templates, the approval status and review history of each post, and the roles of the people in the workspace.
  • Social authorizations: the credential for each platform you connect — OAuth access and refresh tokens, an application password or bot token for WordPress, Telegram, Discord and Ghost, or a Bluesky handle plus app password. Credentials are encrypted before storage and, when you enable cloud publishing, kept in our backend vault so scheduled posts can publish while the app is closed.
  • Inbox data: messages and comments read for channels where you use the inbox, described below.
  • Notifications: an Expo push token per device where you enabled notifications, with the device platform.
  • Billing: Stripe on the web and, where offered, the app store you purchased from. We store a customer and subscription reference, never your card number.

We run no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no cross-app trackers. We do not sell your data.

Connected social accounts

Sosial publishes to 18 channels. We access a connected account only to publish what you schedule, to read the messages and comments you open in the inbox, and to read the profile and statistics fields needed to show results. LinkedIn and Google Business Profile are in preview and not connectable yet, so no data is collected for them.

  • X: read your profile, publish and read posts, upload media, and read and answer direct messages. A refresh token keeps scheduled posts publishing. X requires a paid plan because its posting API is metered.
  • Instagram: read your Business or Creator profile, publish the photos, videos and reels you schedule, read comments, and answer direct messages. We deliberately do not request insights access.
  • TikTok: read your basic profile and video statistics, upload and publish the videos and photos you schedule, and list posted videos to confirm delivery.
  • Facebook: list the Pages you manage, read the posts published on them and the comment count of each, publish scheduled posts, photos and videos, read the comments on those posts, and read and answer Messenger conversations for those Pages. Personal timelines are never touched.
  • Threads: read your profile, publish scheduled posts, read replies, and read post statistics for content published through Sosial.
  • Bluesky: publish scheduled posts and upload their images and video, read and answer direct messages, and read comments, using a short-lived session minted from your app password.
  • Mastodon: read and write access on the instance you choose, verify the account, publish scheduled posts, upload media, and read and answer direct messages and comments. Your instance address is stored so publishing reaches the right server.
  • LinkedIn (preview): once production access is approved, identify you via OpenID, publish posts as you, read your own posts and their statistics, and do the same for a Company Page you administer.
  • YouTube: upload videos to your channel, read the channel and video list to confirm delivery and show status, and read and manage comments on uploads published through Sosial.
  • Pinterest: read your account, and read and write boards and Pins, publishing scheduled Pins to the boards you choose.
  • WordPress: connect a self-hosted or WordPress.com site with an application password, and publish scheduled posts as drafts or live.
  • Dev.to: publish scheduled articles and upload their cover images through the Dev.to API.
  • Hashnode: publish scheduled articles to a publication you own through Hashnode.
  • Ghost: publish scheduled posts to a Ghost site through its Admin API.
  • Telegram: send scheduled messages and media to a channel or group you administer, through a bot you authorise.
  • Discord: post scheduled messages and media to a server channel through a bot you authorise.
  • VK: publish scheduled posts and media to your VK profile or community.
  • Google Business Profile (preview): once Google approves API access, publish scheduled posts to a location you manage.

Some platforms gate features behind their own review — Meta for advanced Page and Instagram permissions, Google for Business Profile, LinkedIn for production API access. Until that approval lands the channel stays in preview and no account is accessed.

Disconnecting a channel deletes the stored credential and pauses publishing. A separate Delete data action erases the posts, stats, comments and messages stored for that channel.

Messages and comments

If you use the inbox or comments features, we read the messages and comments that arrive on the channels you connect so they can be shown in one place and answered from Sosial. That covers direct messages on Facebook Pages (Messenger), Instagram, X, Mastodon and Bluesky, and comments on Facebook, Instagram, Threads, X, Mastodon, Bluesky and YouTube.

  • Conversations and messages: the participant's display name and avatar, the text you and they write, attachments, direction, timestamp and delivery status.
  • Comments: the author's display name and avatar, the text, the post it belongs to, the timestamp, and whether you replied to it.

We read this through each platform's API on a sync schedule and through the platforms' webhooks, only for channels in your workspace, and we store it so the inbox loads without calling the platform on every visit. We do not sell this data, do not use it for advertising, and do not use it to train models. Disconnecting a channel stops new reads but leaves what was already synced until you choose Delete data for that channel.

Notifications

If you enable push notifications, your device registers an Expo push token, stored against your user and workspace with the device platform. We use it to tell you when a post publishes, fails or needs approval, and when your team changes. Delivery runs through Expo's push service; alert text can contain a post title or a teammate's name. You can turn notifications off in the app or in device settings; the token is deleted with your account.

Content sources and AI

Media sources (Dropbox, Canva, Unsplash, OneDrive) are read-only and only the file you pick is ever downloaded. Notion and Google Sheets imports are one-way, into drafts. Automation tools and AI agents act through an API key you control and can revoke. AI features — the writer, per-channel captions, and blog and studio drafts — send your brief and the material you point at to OpenAI, our AI provider; briefs, inputs and drafts are never used to train models. AI output can be wrong, so review it before publishing. Google Drive and Google Photos are not connectable yet, pending Google's verification, and no data is read from them.

How we use it

  • Publish and schedule the posts you create, to the accounts you chose.
  • Show your calendar, queue, inbox, comments and per-channel results.
  • Route posts through approvals and notify the right teammates.
  • Deliver push notifications you enabled and essential service email.
  • Operate your subscription and provide support.

We do not sell your data, we do not use your content to train AI models, and we do not use Google user data to serve advertisements.

Sharing

  • Platforms you connect: publishing inherently sends your post content and media to that platform's API, under your account; inbox reads go to the platforms whose messages and comments you open.
  • Infrastructure providers: Supabase (database, authentication, storage), Vercel (web hosting), Railway (publishing worker) and Expo (push delivery) process data on our behalf.
  • OpenAI: receives the brief and material you submit to an AI feature and returns the result.
  • Billing: Stripe for web subscriptions, and the app store for mobile purchases where offered.
  • Source tools you connect: Dropbox, Canva, Unsplash, OneDrive, Notion, Google Sheets, Zapier and MCP, called only for the item you pick or the action you trigger.
  • We disclose data if required by law or to protect rights and safety; where lawful we tell you first. Nothing else is shared.

Retention

  • Credentials are kept until you disconnect the channel or delete your account.
  • Media attached to scheduled posts is kept until the post publishes (plus short retry windows), then cleaned up; your media library stays until you delete it.
  • Sent-post records are kept so your history and analytics work, until you delete them or your account.
  • Accounts inactive for six consecutive months (no sign-in and no activity) are deleted along with all their data. A reminder email is sent one month before, and signing in at any point cancels the deletion. A shared workspace is only deleted once every member has been inactive — any teammate signing in or posting keeps the workspace and its data. Support tickets and their screenshots are erased 30 days after the ticket is resolved. Active paid accounts are never deleted for inactivity.

Marketing emails

We send product news and tips only to people who opted in — via the optional checkbox at signup or a preference in account settings. Every marketing email carries a one-click unsubscribe link that takes effect immediately. Your email address is never shared with advertising networks, and unsubscribing does not affect operational emails such as security notices, receipts or publishing confirmations.

Your choices

  • Disconnect any channel in Connect to revoke its credentials.
  • Delete data for a single channel without disconnecting it.
  • Delete your account in the app, or request deletion at sosial.app/delete-data.
  • Turn push notifications off in the app or in device settings.
  • Contact us for a copy of your data.

Security

Credentials are held in device secure storage and a server-side vault, encrypted with AES-256-GCM, never in plain app code or logs. Traffic is encrypted in transit, data is encrypted at rest, and production access is limited and logged. No method is perfect, but we limit access to what publishing and the inbox need.

Children

Sosial is a business tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone below the minimum age required to hold a social media account in their jurisdiction.
