Privacy Policy

Effective: September 19, 2026 · Sosial (“we”, “the app”)

What Sosial does

Sosial lets you compose posts and publish or schedule them to social accounts you connect (Facebook, Instagram, Threads, TikTok, X, Bluesky, Mastodon, LinkedIn, Pinterest, YouTube). This policy explains what data that requires and how we handle it.

Data we collect

  • Account info: your email address, used for sign-in and team identity.
  • Social authorizations: OAuth access and refresh tokens for each platform you connect. Tokens live in your device's secure storage and, when you enable cloud publishing, in our backend vault so scheduled posts can publish while the app is closed.
  • Your content: drafts, captions, schedules, and the photos/videos you attach. Media for scheduled posts is mirrored to our backend storage so it can publish on time.
  • Team roster: names, emails, and roles you add, used only for the approvals workflow.

We run no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no cross-app trackers. We do not sell your data.

How we use it

  • Publish and schedule the posts you create, to the accounts you chose.
  • Show per-channel stats and comments you ask to see.
  • Keep you signed in and your scheduled queue running.

Sharing

  • Platforms you connect: publishing inherently sends your post content and media to that platform's API, under your account.
  • Hosting providers: Supabase (database, auth, storage) processes data on our behalf.
  • Your TikTok photo host: if you configure one for TikTok photo posts, images pass through a server you control; our reference setup deletes them automatically after 24 hours.
  • We disclose data if required by law. Nothing else is shared.

Retention

  • Tokens are kept until you disconnect the channel or delete your account.
  • Scheduled media is kept until the post publishes (plus short retry windows), then cleaned up.
  • Sent-post records are kept so your history and analytics work, until you delete them or your account.

Your choices

  • Disconnect any channel in Connect to revoke its tokens.
  • Delete your account in the app — this wipes local data and tokens.
  • Contact us (below) for a copy or deletion of backend data.

Security

Tokens are held in device secure storage and a server-side vault, never in plain app code or logs. No method is perfect, but we limit access to what publishing needs.

Children

Sosial is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will update this page when the policy changes and revise the effective date above.
