# Privacy Policy — Sosial

**Effective: October 27, 2026.** Operated by EGATE WORLDWIDE.

Sosial is a scheduling and publishing service for social media. You connect
accounts, compose and schedule posts, and Sosial publishes them through each
platform's official API. This is the short version of
[the full policy](https://sosial.app/privacy).

## What we store

- **Account:** your email address and an encrypted password, or your OAuth
  identity if you signed in with a provider.
- **Workspace:** posts, captions, schedules, media, templates, approval status
  and review history, and the roles of the people in the workspace.
- **Channel credentials:** an OAuth token pair, an application password or bot
  token for WordPress/Telegram/Discord/Ghost, or a Bluesky handle plus app
  password. Encrypted before storage; decrypted only when a request needs them.
- **Inbox:** messages and comments, only for the channels where you use the
  inbox.
- **Notifications:** an Expo push token per device where you enabled them.
- **Billing:** a customer and subscription reference from Stripe or the app
  store. Never your card number.

## Connected platforms

18 channels: X, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Threads, YouTube, LinkedIn,
Bluesky, Mastodon, Pinterest, Telegram, Discord, WordPress, Dev.to, Hashnode,
Ghost, VK and Google Business Profile. LinkedIn and Google Business Profile are
in preview and not connectable yet, so no data is collected for them.

We access a connected account only to publish what you schedule, to read the
messages and comments you open in the inbox, and to read the profile and
statistics fields needed to show results. We do not read anything on a platform
you have not connected, and personal Facebook timelines are never touched.
The full per-platform list is in [the policy](https://sosial.app/privacy).

## Bluesky

Bluesky has no OAuth, so it uses a handle and an app password. That password is
the one password-shaped credential we keep: encrypted in our vault and in your
device's secure storage, never shown back to you, never logged, and used only
to mint short-lived sessions for publishing. Disconnecting deletes it.

## AI

The writer, per-channel captions and the blog and studio drafts send your brief
to OpenAI, our AI provider. We do not use your inputs to train models, and
OpenAI does not train on data submitted through its API. Review AI output
before publishing.

## What we never do

No advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, no cross-app trackers, no sale of your
data, no use of your content to train models, and no use of Google user data
for advertising.

## Retention

Credentials live until you disconnect a channel or delete your account.
Workspace data lives while the account is active. Accounts inactive for six
consecutive months are deleted, with an email reminder one month before and
sign-in cancelling it; shared workspaces need every member to be inactive, and
paid accounts are never deleted for inactivity. Support tickets are erased 30
days after resolution.

## Your choices

Disconnect a channel to revoke its credentials, Delete data for one channel
without disconnecting it, turn push off in the app or device settings, or
delete your account in the app or at [sosial.app/delete-data](https://sosial.app/delete-data).
